KNOW WHAT YOU SHARE

A clear answer to “what happens to my data?”

Your ID photo

The current demo runs OCR in your browser. The photo is not uploaded; only the date you save is sent to your profile. OCR does not check whether an ID is genuine or belongs to you.

Your account and date

Supabase Auth stores your email and password hash. A date saved from OCR is marked unverified and does not determine website access. Provider verification, when configured, is stored separately.

Your browsing pass

A real pass contains a signed age band, expiry, credential ID, format version, and public device key. It does not contain your birthdate or account ID. OCR and demo results do not issue a real pass.

A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Before you
head out.

Getting stuck is normal.
Here’s how to get moving again.

Does this work on every website?+

This build checks top-level visits to the domains in its small rating list. Sites outside that list open normally. It works only in Chrome while the extension is installed and enabled; another browser can bypass it.

What if I don’t have an ID?+

Choose the child-limited path. It assigns the most restrictive Under 13 pass; this is not proof of your actual age, and all rated sites requiring 13+ remain blocked. You can add an ID check later.

Does uploading an ID verify my age?+

No. The demo reads a DOB with browser OCR and saves it as unverified. Demo verification is simulated; it does not authenticate an ID or issue an age pass.

Why does a rated site remain locked?+

A real age pass requires a provider-verified profile. OCR and demo results do not activate the extension pass.
